About LabRecord

LabRecord is an online platform providing free and practical digital tools, calculators and informational resources designed to make everyday tasks simpler and more accessible.

What is LabRecord?

LabRecord brings together a collection of browser-based tools and calculators that users can access without installing software. The platform is designed to provide simple interfaces for common calculations and everyday digital tasks.

Our tools include calculators for percentages, age, EMI, GST, discounts, unit conversion and other everyday requirements.

LabRecord also provides a separate Finance section containing calculators related to SIPs, fixed deposits, compound interest, loans, inflation, retirement planning and lumpsum investments.

Our Approach

We focus on making our tools straightforward, accessible and easy to understand. Calculations are presented in a simple format so that users can enter their information and understand the resulting estimates.

LabRecord is continuously improved based on usability, accuracy and the needs of people using the platform.

Free Online Tools

Our Tools section contains free browser-based utilities and calculators for everyday use. These tools are intended to save time and provide convenient calculations directly from a web browser.

Finance Resources

The Finance section provides calculators that can be used to understand mathematical estimates involving investments, loans, interest, inflation and retirement planning.

Financial calculator results are estimates based on the information entered by the user. They should not be treated as personalized financial advice or guaranteed investment outcomes.
