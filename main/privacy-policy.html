PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LabRecord handles information when you visit or use our website, online tools and contact form.

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. About This Policy

LabRecord ("LabRecord", "we", "us" or "our") operates www.labrecord.cloud, including our online tools and finance calculator sections.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect only information that is reasonably necessary to operate the website, respond to enquiries and improve our services.

2. Information We Collect

Most LabRecord tools can be used without creating an account. We do not require visitors to provide their name, phone number or email address merely to browse the website or use ordinary calculators.

Information may be provided voluntarily when you use our Contact page. This may include:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The subject of your enquiry
  • The message or other information you choose to submit

3. Contact Form

Our contact form uses Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service, to transmit contact form submissions to our designated email inbox. Web3Forms states that its service processes form submissions and forwards them to the configured email destination. Its documentation also describes server-side spam protection and handling of form data.

Please do not submit passwords, one-time passwords (OTPs), payment card numbers, bank account credentials, medical records or other highly sensitive information through the contact form.

4. How We Use Information

Information submitted through the contact form may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Provide support regarding our website or tools
  • Review feedback and suggestions
  • Investigate reported technical issues
  • Respond to legitimate business enquiries

We do not use contact form information for unrelated purposes simply because it was submitted to us.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

LabRecord may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for website functionality, security, analytics or advertising. Some cookies may be placed by third-party services rather than directly by LabRecord.

6. Advertising and Google AdSense

LabRecord may display advertising, including Google AdSense, if advertising services are enabled on the website.

When Google advertising is used, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve, measure or personalize advertisements based on applicable settings and user activity. Google requires publishers using its advertising products to disclose relevant cookie and third-party advertising practices in their privacy policies.

Users can manage Google's advertising personalization settings through Google's advertising controls. Applicable consent requirements may also apply depending on a user's location.

Important: The exact advertising technologies and partners used on LabRecord may change as our advertising setup changes. This policy will be updated when material changes are made.

7. Analytics and Technical Information

Our hosting or third-party services may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, timestamps and similar information as part of normal website operation, security, performance or diagnostics.

We do not intentionally request sensitive personal information from visitors through ordinary website pages or calculators.

8. Financial Calculators

LabRecord's financial calculators are designed to provide mathematical estimates based on information entered by the user. Information entered into a calculator should not be treated as a recommendation or financial advice.

Where a calculator operates entirely in the browser, the values entered may be processed locally by the page. Users should still avoid entering confidential information into any website tool.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

LabRecord may link to or use third-party services, websites, advertising networks or technology providers. These third parties have their own privacy policies and terms.

LabRecord is not responsible for the privacy practices of websites that it does not operate. We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies of third-party services before providing them with personal information.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through our website and services. However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For this reason, please do not send confidential credentials, financial account passwords, OTPs or similarly sensitive data through the website's contact form.

11. Data Retention

Contact information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse or comply with applicable obligations.

Third-party service providers may have their own retention periods and policies for information they process.

12. Your Choices and Requests

If you have submitted personal information through our contact form and want to ask about that information, correct it or request deletion where applicable, you may contact us through the Contact page.

Requests may be subject to reasonable verification and to any legal or operational requirements that apply.

13. Children's Privacy

LabRecord is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly request sensitive personal information from children through our tools or contact form.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, advertising arrangements or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how LabRecord handles information, please use our Contact page.

We do not publicly display our direct email address on this page.
