Free Inflation Calculator

Inflation reduces the purchasing power of money over time. This calculator estimates how much money may be required in the future to purchase something that costs a certain amount today.

Inflation Formula

The estimated future cost can be calculated using compound inflation:

Future Cost = Current Cost × (1 + Inflation Rate)ⁿ

For example, if something costs ₹1,00,000 today and inflation averages 6% per year, its estimated future cost after 10 years would be higher.

Why Inflation Matters

  • It reduces the purchasing power of money.
  • Future expenses can be higher than today's expenses.
  • Long-term financial planning should consider inflation.
  • Retirement planning often accounts for future inflation.
  • Education, healthcare and living costs can change over time.

Example

Suppose an expense costs ₹1,00,000 today and inflation averages 6% annually. After 10 years, the estimated equivalent cost would be approximately ₹1,79,085.

Disclaimer: This calculator uses an assumed inflation rate to provide a mathematical estimate. Actual inflation varies over time and between products, services and locations. This tool is for educational and planning purposes and is not financial advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an inflation calculator show?

It estimates how the cost of goods or services may change over time if a specified annual inflation rate continues.

What inflation rate should I use?

There is no single rate that applies to every future expense. You can enter an assumption appropriate for your own planning scenario.

Does inflation reduce purchasing power?

Yes. When prices rise, the same amount of money generally buys fewer goods and services than before.

Are inflation estimates guaranteed?

No. Inflation can change from year to year, so the result should be treated as an estimate rather than a prediction.
