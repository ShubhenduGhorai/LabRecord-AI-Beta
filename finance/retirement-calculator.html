Free Retirement Calculator

The LabRecord Retirement Calculator provides an estimate of how your current expenses, inflation, existing savings and future investments could affect your retirement planning.

How Retirement Corpus Is Estimated

First, the calculator estimates your monthly expenses at retirement by applying the assumed inflation rate for the years remaining until retirement.

Future Expense = Current Expense × (1 + Inflation Rate)ⁿ

It then estimates the retirement corpus required to support the selected number of retirement years using the same assumed annual return.

How Your Savings Are Projected

Existing retirement savings are compounded over the years until retirement. Monthly investments are also added throughout the accumulation period and assumed to grow at the selected annual return.

Steps to Use the Calculator

  1. Enter your current age.
  2. Enter your expected retirement age.
  3. Enter your current monthly expenses.
  4. Enter an assumed inflation rate.
  5. Enter your existing retirement savings.
  6. Enter your current monthly investment.
  7. Enter an assumed annual investment return.
  8. Enter the number of years you expect to need retirement funds.

Important Limitations

  • Actual investment returns can vary significantly.
  • Inflation may not remain constant.
  • Taxes, fees and investment costs are not included.
  • Actual retirement expenses can differ from estimates.
  • The calculator does not account for every possible income source or financial obligation.
Disclaimer: This calculator provides simplified mathematical estimates for educational and planning purposes only. It does not guarantee investment returns or retirement outcomes and should not be considered personalized financial advice.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a retirement corpus?

A retirement corpus is the amount of savings and investments accumulated to help fund expenses after retirement.

Why is inflation important for retirement planning?

Inflation can increase the cost of everyday expenses over time. Therefore, expenses that seem affordable today may require more money in the future.

Are the calculator's returns guaranteed?

No. The return rate is only an assumption used for the mathematical projection.

Does this calculator include pension income?

No. This basic calculator focuses on expenses, savings and investment growth. Other income sources should be considered separately.
