How Loan EMI Is Calculated

For a standard reducing-balance loan, the monthly payment can be calculated using the following formula:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Example

Suppose you borrow ₹5,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 8.5% for 5 years. The calculator estimates the monthly payment and shows the total interest payable over the loan period.

Factors That Affect Loan Payments

  • Repayment frequency

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a loan EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the regular payment made toward a loan, generally consisting of both principal and interest.

Does this calculator include processing fees?

No. This calculator estimates principal and interest payments. Actual borrowing costs may also include processing fees, insurance, taxes or other charges.

Can I use this for a home loan?

Yes. The calculator can provide a basic estimate for home loans, personal loans, car loans and other standard amortizing loans.

Is this calculator financial advice?

No. The results are estimates for educational and planning purposes. Actual loan terms depend on the lender and your specific agreement.
