What is a SIP?

SIP stands for Systematic Investment Plan. It allows an investor to invest a fixed amount regularly, usually every month, into a mutual fund scheme. Instead of investing a large amount at once, SIP spreads the investment over a longer period.

How does a SIP Calculator work?

A SIP calculator estimates the potential future value of regular investments using the monthly investment amount, expected annual return and investment duration.

The calculation assumes that the expected rate of return remains constant throughout the investment period. Actual mutual fund returns can be higher or lower.

SIP Calculator Formula

FV = P × [((1 + r)n − 1) / r] × (1 + r)

Where:

  • FV = Estimated future value
  • P = Monthly investment
  • r = Monthly rate of return
  • n = Total number of monthly investments

Example of a SIP Investment

Suppose you invest ₹5,000 every month for 10 years and assume an annual return of 12%. Your total contribution would be ₹6,00,000. At the assumed rate, the calculator estimates the potential future value based on monthly compounding.

Benefits of SIP

  • Encourages regular investing.
  • Allows investments with relatively small amounts.
  • Can help build a long-term investment habit.
  • Compounding can increase the potential value over longer periods.
  • Regular investments can reduce dependence on timing a single investment.

SIP vs Lump Sum Investment

In a SIP, money is invested periodically, whereas a lump sum investment involves investing a larger amount at one time. Which approach is appropriate depends on factors such as cash flow, investment horizon, risk tolerance and market conditions.
