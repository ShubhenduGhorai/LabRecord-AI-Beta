How Does an Age Calculator Work?

An age calculator determines the amount of time that has passed between your date of birth and today's date. It calculates the result using years, months and days rather than simply dividing the number of days by 365.

How to Calculate Age

To calculate someone's age, start with their date of birth and compare it with the current date.

Age = Current Date − Date of Birth

The calculation takes into account the different number of days in each month and leap years.

How Many Days Old Am I?

Your total age in days is calculated by finding the number of complete days between your date of birth and the current date.

Because calendar years can contain 365 or 366 days, the exact number of days depends on the dates involved.

How Many Months Old Am I?

Your total months can be estimated from the number of complete years and months that have passed since your date of birth.

For example, someone who is 25 years and 6 months old has completed approximately 306 months of age.

What Is My Next Birthday?

Your next birthday is the next occurrence of your birth date after today's date. The calculator also tells you how many days remain until that birthday.

Why Are Leap Years Important?

A normal calendar year has 365 days, while a leap year has 366 days. Leap years can therefore affect calculations involving total days and dates.

Frequently Asked Questions
