How to Calculate a Discount

To calculate the discount amount, multiply the original price by the discount percentage.

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

The final sale price is then calculated by subtracting the discount amount from the original price.

Sale Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example

If a product costs ₹1,000 and has a 20% discount, the discount is ₹200 and the sale price is ₹800.

Common Discount Examples

  • 10% discount on ₹1,000 = ₹100 savings
  • 20% discount on ₹1,000 = ₹200 savings
  • 30% discount on ₹2,000 = ₹600 savings
  • 50% discount on ₹5,000 = ₹2,500 savings

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate 20% off a price?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100 to find the discount amount. Subtract that amount from the original price.

What is the difference between discount and sale price?

The discount is the amount saved, while the sale price is the amount paid after the discount.

Can I calculate GST after a discount?

Yes. Select a GST rate and the calculator will estimate GST on the price after discount. Actual tax treatment can depend on the transaction and applicable rules.
