What Is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter is an online tool that converts measurements from one unit to another. It can be useful when working with different measurement systems in everyday life, education, business, engineering and other activities.

Length Conversion

Length conversions are useful for converting measurements such as millimeters, centimeters, meters, kilometers, inches, feet, yards and miles.

1 meter = 100 centimeters

Weight Conversion

Weight and mass conversions can be used to convert between kilograms, grams, milligrams, pounds and ounces.

1 kilogram = 1000 grams

Temperature Conversion

Temperature can be expressed using Celsius, Fahrenheit and Kelvin scales.

Fahrenheit = (Celsius × 9/5) + 32

Area Conversion

Area conversion is useful when working with measurements such as square meters, square kilometers, square feet and acres.

Volume Conversion

Volume units can be used to measure liquids and three-dimensional quantities. Common units include liters, milliliters, cubic meters and gallons.

Digital Storage Conversion

Digital storage can be expressed using units such as bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes and terabytes.

Why Use an Online Unit Converter?

An online converter can save time and reduce manual calculation when you need to work with unfamiliar measurement units. The result can be calculated directly in your browser without installing software.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert meters to feet?

Select Length, choose meters as the source unit and feet as the destination unit. Enter the value you want to convert and the result will be calculated.

How many centimeters are in one meter?

One meter contains 100 centimeters.

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9/5 and add 32. The calculator performs this conversion automatically.

How many grams are in one kilogram?

One kilogram equals 1,000 grams.

Can I convert digital storage units?

Yes. This calculator supports bytes, kilobytes, megabytes, gigabytes and terabytes.
