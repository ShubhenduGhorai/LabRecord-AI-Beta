How to Calculate a Percentage

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. Percentages are commonly used in mathematics, shopping discounts, finance, business and everyday calculations.

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

How to Calculate X% of a Number

To calculate a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100.

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

For example, 20% of 500 is 100.

How to Calculate Percentage Change

Percentage change measures how much a value has increased or decreased compared with its original value.

Percentage Change = ((New Value − Original Value) ÷ Original Value) × 100

If a price increases from ₹500 to ₹600, the percentage increase is 20%.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is 10% of 100?

10% of 100 is 10.

What is 20% of 500?

20% of 500 is 100.

How do I calculate a percentage increase?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the result by the original value and multiply by 100.
