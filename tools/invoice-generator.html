Free Invoice Generator Online

LabRecord Invoice Generator helps small businesses, freelancers and service providers create simple professional invoices online.

Enter your business details, customer information, invoice items and applicable GST rate. You can then print the invoice or choose the PDF option in your browser's print dialog.

How to Create an Invoice

  1. Enter your business information.
  2. Add your customer details.
  3. Add products or services.
  4. Enter quantity and price.
  5. Select the applicable GST rate if required.
  6. Click Print / Save PDF.

Who Can Use This Invoice Generator?

  • Freelancers
  • Small businesses
  • Consultants
  • Online sellers
  • Service providers
  • Independent professionals

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this invoice generator free?

Yes. You can create and print invoices using the tool without paying for the generator.

Can I save the invoice as PDF?

Yes. Click Print / Save PDF and select "Save as PDF" in your browser's print dialog.

Does the invoice generator upload my information?

The invoice is generated in your browser. The information entered into the form is not required to be uploaded to a server.

Can I add GST?

Yes. You can select common GST rates from the GST dropdown. Always verify the applicable GST rate and invoicing requirements for your particular transaction.
