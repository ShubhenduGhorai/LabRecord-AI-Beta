What Is an EMI?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the fixed amount a borrower generally pays every month toward a loan. Each payment includes both principal and interest.

How Is EMI Calculated?

The standard EMI formula uses the loan principal, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly payments.

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ ÷ ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1)

Where P is the principal loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate and n is the total number of monthly payments.

Example of EMI Calculation

Suppose you borrow ₹5,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 8.5% for 5 years. The calculator converts the annual rate into a monthly rate and calculates the corresponding monthly EMI.

What Does Your EMI Include?

Your monthly EMI normally consists of two components: principal repayment and interest. At the beginning of many amortizing loans, a larger portion of the payment goes toward interest. As the loan balance decreases, the interest component generally decreases.

How Can I Reduce My Loan EMI?

A lower interest rate, a longer repayment period or borrowing a smaller amount can reduce the monthly EMI. However, extending the tenure can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

EMI vs Total Interest

A lower monthly EMI does not necessarily mean a cheaper loan. If the repayment period is significantly longer, the total interest paid can be considerably higher.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an EMI calculator?

An EMI calculator is an online tool that estimates the monthly payment, total interest and total repayment for a loan.

Can I use this calculator for a home loan?

Yes. You can use the calculator to estimate EMI for home loans, personal loans, vehicle loans and other amortizing loans.

Does a longer loan tenure reduce EMI?

Generally, a longer tenure reduces the monthly EMI, but it can increase the total amount of interest paid over the loan period.

What happens if the interest rate increases?

For a loan with a variable interest rate, an increase in the applicable rate can increase the EMI, extend the repayment period or both, depending on the lender's terms.

Is the EMI result an exact bank quote?

No. This calculator provides an estimate based on the values entered. Actual loan payments can differ because of lender-specific rates, fees, insurance, taxes and other charges.
