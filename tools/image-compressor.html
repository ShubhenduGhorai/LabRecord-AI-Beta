JPG vs WebP vs PNG

JPG: Good for photographs and images where a smaller file size is important.

WebP: A modern image format that can provide smaller files while maintaining good visual quality.

PNG: Useful when transparency or sharper graphics are important.

Why Compress Images?

  • Improve page loading performance.
  • Save storage space.
  • Make images easier to share online.
  • Reduce unnecessary bandwidth usage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this image compressor free?

Yes. You can use the tool without paying for image compression.

Are my images uploaded?

No. Image processing is performed locally in your browser.

Which image formats are supported?

The tool accepts JPG, PNG and WebP images.

Does lower quality mean a smaller file?

Lower quality generally produces a smaller file for JPG and WebP, but the final size depends on the image.

Is image quality reduced?

Compression can reduce image quality. Use a higher quality setting when you want to preserve more detail.
