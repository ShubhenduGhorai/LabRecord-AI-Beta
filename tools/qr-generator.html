What Is a QR Code?

A QR code, or Quick Response code, is a two-dimensional barcode that can store information such as website addresses, text, contact information and other data.

How Does a QR Code Work?

A QR code represents information using a pattern of dark and light modules. A compatible camera or QR scanner can read the pattern and decode the stored information.

What Can You Put in a QR Code?

You can use QR codes for website URLs, plain text, contact details, phone numbers, email addresses, Wi-Fi information and many other types of data.

Where Are QR Codes Used?

QR codes are commonly used on restaurant menus, product packaging, posters, business cards, advertisements, event tickets and payment-related applications.

Are QR Codes Free to Create?

Static QR codes can be generated without paying a recurring fee. This tool creates a QR code directly in your browser.

Static vs Dynamic QR Codes

A static QR code contains the destination information directly in the code. A dynamic QR code typically uses a redirecting service, allowing the destination to be changed later and often providing analytics.

QR Code Safety

A QR code itself does not guarantee that the destination is safe. Before opening a QR code link, check the destination address and avoid entering sensitive information on unfamiliar websites.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I create a QR code for a website?

Yes. Enter the complete website address into the text box and generate the QR code.

Can I download the QR code?

Yes. After generating the QR code, use the download button to save it as a PNG image.

Can a QR code contain plain text?

Yes. You can encode ordinary text as well as URLs and other supported information.

Does a QR code expire?

A static QR code generally does not have an expiration date. However, a URL encoded inside it may stop working if the destination website is removed or changed.

Can I use the QR code commercially?

You can use generated QR images for many purposes, but make sure that the content or destination you encode complies with the applicable terms and laws.
