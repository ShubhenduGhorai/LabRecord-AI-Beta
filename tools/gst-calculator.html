What Is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax applied to the supply of many goods and services in India.

The applicable GST rate depends on the particular goods or services and the relevant tax classification.

How to Calculate GST

When GST is added to a price, the GST amount can be calculated by multiplying the base amount by the GST rate.

GST Amount = Base Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100
Final Price = Base Amount + GST Amount

Example: Adding 18% GST

If the base price of a product is ₹10,000 and the applicable GST rate is 18%, the GST amount is ₹1,800 and the price including GST is ₹11,800.

How to Remove GST From an Inclusive Price

If a displayed price already includes GST, you cannot simply subtract the GST percentage from that price. The GST-inclusive amount must be converted back to its taxable base amount.

Base Amount = Inclusive Price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)
GST Amount = Inclusive Price − Base Amount

What Are CGST and SGST?

For an eligible intra-state transaction, GST can be divided between Central GST (CGST) and State GST (SGST). When the applicable GST rate is split equally, an 18% GST rate corresponds to 9% CGST and 9% SGST.

What Is IGST?

Integrated GST (IGST) generally applies to eligible inter-state supplies. The applicable tax treatment depends on the transaction and the relevant GST rules.

Important GST Note

This calculator is intended for general estimation and educational purposes. GST rates and tax treatment can depend on the product, service, transaction type, location and applicable rules. Always verify the applicable GST rate before using the result for accounting, invoicing or tax filing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the GST formula?

GST amount can be calculated as base amount multiplied by the applicable GST rate divided by 100.

How do I calculate 18% GST?

Multiply the taxable amount by 18 and divide by 100. For example, 18% of ₹10,000 is ₹1,800.

How do I remove GST from an inclusive price?

Divide the GST-inclusive price by 1 plus the GST rate expressed as a decimal. For an 18% rate, divide the inclusive amount by 1.18.

Is CGST always equal to SGST?

For eligible intra-state transactions where the applicable GST is evenly split, CGST and SGST are equal halves of the total GST rate.

Can I use this GST calculator for invoices?

You can use it to estimate GST amounts, but actual invoices should use the correct tax classification, rate and transaction details applicable to the sale.
