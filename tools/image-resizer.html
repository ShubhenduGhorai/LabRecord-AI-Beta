Free Online Image Resizer

LabRecord Image Resizer lets you change the dimensions of JPG, PNG and WebP images directly in your browser. You can specify the width and height and download the resized image.

Your image is processed locally in your browser, so you do not need to upload it to a server.

How to Resize an Image

  1. Click "Choose an Image".
  2. Select a JPG, PNG or WebP image.
  3. Enter the desired width and height.
  4. Keep "Keep aspect ratio" enabled if you want to maintain the original proportions.
  5. Select the output format and quality.
  6. Click "Resize Image".
  7. Download the resized image.

Why Resize Images?

  • Prepare images for websites.
  • Reduce image dimensions for social media.
  • Create smaller images for email attachments.
  • Meet image dimension requirements for online forms.
  • Reduce unnecessary image dimensions.

Image Resizer vs Image Compressor

An image resizer changes the physical dimensions of an image, such as reducing it from 4000 × 3000 pixels to 1200 × 900 pixels.

An image compressor primarily reduces the file size while attempting to maintain the image dimensions. For the best result, resizing and compression can sometimes be used together.
